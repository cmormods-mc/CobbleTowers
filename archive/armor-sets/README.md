# Armor sets (archived for now)

The armor pieces no longer drop: their entries are gone from the four `reward_tables` (the originals are in `reward_tables/` here).
Kept in the mod so pieces players already hold still work: the item registry, the `armor_sets` datapack files, the worn models and the
`/armor` command. Their names carry "(WIP)" in `lang/en_us.json`.

To bring them back: copy these tables over `src/main/resources/data/cobbletowers/cobbletowers/reward_tables/` and drop " (WIP)" from the lang names.
