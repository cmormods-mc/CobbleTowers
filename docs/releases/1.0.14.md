# CobbleTowers 1.0.14

Covers everything since 1.0.10. The in-tree builds 1.0.11 (co-op boss roster fix), 1.0.12 (camera and battle exit fixes) and 1.0.14 (goblin merchant) were never released on their own, so this one note replaces three. Ship it with the matching AscensionLib jar (the Mystery Bag needs `AscensionGrants.grantAsVouchers`, AscensionLib#7) and the CobbleRaids build that main publishes (0.8.204-mew-model-fix).

## Hollow's Eve, the physical tower

- A generated 10-floor walkable building: gothic battle hall, three route doors behind pedestals that light up and open on the vote, eight stop rooms dressed per lane, a stair of its own for every route lane, a floating-island exterior, pumpkin lighting.
- A picked pedestal opens the door it stands in front of. The stair gate opens by itself once no modifier vote is open (no ready-up); stragglers are taken after 5 minutes. A second click copy on a pedestal is ignored.
- Route map for the event tower: `/cobbletowers play route` opens it on request at any point of a run, never on a floor clear (CobbleTowers `feature/bridge-hud`). The route book and the Route Map hotbar item remain.
- Trick or Treat vault (P55): even-odds treat or mimic. A lost mimic takes a held relic and 5% of the final loot (taken first, returned on a win). Event towers only.
- Haunted rare: a treat can hold a catchable Halloween Pokémon (seeded 15% chance, three throws with pity odds, the catch goes to the PC, 2 per player per day), with a click-through tray on the overlay.
- Halloween-skinned bosses in the floor pools, Mimikyu on floor 5 and Mew on floor 10; disguise scouting from floor 3 shows a seeded decoy species and hides typing.
- Grizelda the Goblin Merchant (a Blockbench model behind a runtime booth); the vendor's patter by floor.
- Mystery Bag: a physical, tradable item you right-click to open. Two bags in three hold dust, the good materials are very rare. The prize is paid as vouchers.
- Hollow's Eve mastery track: 20 levels (World Boss candies, balls, potions, modest cash, no perks), Spooky Surge Candy Corn at 12 of them. Candy Corn from the Treat is 5 + 2 per floor, capped at 100 a day.
- The window runs to Sunday 8 Nov 11:59 PM Eastern (`2026-11-09T05:00:00Z`).

## Balance

- Phase 0: tower Raid Points cut; run-length telemetry.
- Phase 2: Threat bands on tower runs, boss-rush towers (the default for every tower), a modifier pass, and a Threat picker in the lobby.
- Towers are boss-only: the basic-battle path (waves, encounter pools, enemy level offset, extra opponents) is gone; Echo Duels and vault mimics draw from the boss pool. P58 boss phases are wired through the milestone JSON (Regieleki gets Overclock and Overload).

## Fixes

- Co-op boss roster after a participant leaves; shared battle exit recovery; camera obstructions in tower battles.
- The room behind a chosen lane was overwritten; a relic or lock-in floor opened onto the bare dark shell; wall aging replaced the bars of gate A and the doors.
- The second copy of one pedestal click voted "leave it shut" on a vault vote that opened in the same tick.
- Haunted rare: the catch is counted before it is granted and undone if the grant fails.
- The Mystery Bag's limit of 2 is per player of a run (`max_purchases_per_player`), no longer shared by the whole team: one player's bags used up the cap and a teammate could not buy any. Other services keep the per-run limit.
- Nobody changes the tower while in it: in the tower dimension a player cannot break or place blocks, use a bucket, flint, a spawn egg, a boat, a minecart, bone meal, an axe, shovel, hoe or shears on the world, or break, rotate or fill a painting, an item frame or an armor stand. Pedestals, the vendor, chests and doors are unaffected (a click on a block with one of those items in hand is refused). An operator in creative mode is exempt. Checked on the rig by the owner.
- After a vendor purchase the bridge no longer opens the whole intermission menu behind the shop (bridge fix, SnobbleGUIOverhaul).

## Screens

Players with the Snobblemon client see the tower screens through the bridge (SnobbleGUIOverhaul#1) instead of WebGUI.
