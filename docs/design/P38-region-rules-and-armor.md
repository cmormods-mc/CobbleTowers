# P38: region rules and three armor sets per tower

Status: design, written before the code (the TDS gate). Owner answers 2026-10-08; the numbers below are my proposals for the owner to review before stage 2.

## Decisions already made

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| F10 chase | Keep the God Pack Ticket guaranteed; **armor pieces drop alongside it** (option C: a high-weight bonus-pool entry) |
| Armor scope | **Four towers, three sets each = 12 sets** (48 pieces). The four sets that exist become one of each tower's three |
| How a tower's three differ | **All of tier, role and sub-theme at once** (see the table) |
| Tideforge rule | No held items for the player's Pokemon |
| Rootvale rule | Enemy Pokemon heal a share of the damage they deal: **10% at floor 1 rising to 20% at floor 10** |
| Duskvale rule | Each floor puts an unavoidable status on the **lead** Pokemon at the start of **every fight of the floor** (opponents and boss), the status random per floor (seeded), to be answered with cure items |
| Raid Points | Unchanged (25 / 75) |

## Stage 1: region rules (needs code, no art)

Each is a ruleset (a new `rulesets/<tower>.json`, pointed at by its tower). A ruleset gains three optional fields, all off by default so `standard` and every
saved run are unchanged:

| Field | Meaning | Mechanism (all in the one Showdown extension, `tower-fx.js`, as P23) |
|---|---|---|
| `player_held_items: false` | The player's side ignores held items for the whole battle (no Leftovers, berries, Choice items, Life Orb). The Pokemon keep the item in their party; nothing is removed | a `suppress_items` operation wrapping `Pokemon.ignoringItem` for the named side only, so it is inert for the other side and for any battle without it |
| `enemy_drain_percent` (start and end) | After an enemy Pokemon deals damage it heals that share of it, capped at what it is missing | a `drain` operation: a rule on the named side, applied where damage is dealt (as the existing damage rules are), bounded and fail-open like them |
| `floor_status` (list of statuses) | At the start of every fight of a floor, that floor's status (chosen from the list by the run seed and the floor) is put on the lead; one that already has a status keeps it | the existing `status` operation, with the choice made on the Java side from `seed` and `floorIndex` so a replay gives the same status |

* Java: `RulesetDefinition` parses and validates the fields, `RulesetView` exposes them (the API boundary validator guards the new signatures), and the battle-effects builder adds the operations
  to the battle's `towerFx`. The drain percent is `lerp(10, 20, (floor - 1) / (floorCount - 1))`, rounded, so a tower with another floor count still ends at 20.
* Duskvale statuses: poison, burn, paralysis, sleep, freeze (`psn`, `brn`, `par`, `slp`, `frz`). Applied at the start of every fight of the floor, to the lead only; a Pokemon that already has a status keeps it, so a cure used between fights is answered by the next fight (owner decision 2026-10-08).
  The vendor must sell the matching cures (Antidote, Burn Heal, Paralyze Heal, Awakening, Ice Heal, Full Heal); a Duskvale vendor check is part of the stage's test. Battle items can be used in ordinary
  floor fights; a boss fight under the no-items rule cannot, which is part of the pressure (the owner can relax it).
* Hardcore (no vendor, no items) with Duskvale is the harshest combination and is allowed; it is not special-cased.
* Tests: `validation/showdown/` simulator tests for `suppress_items` (an item-using set loses its effect, the other side keeps it), `drain` (heals the stated share, never over max HP) and the
  deterministic status choice; unit tests for the ruleset parse and the drain curve; a live smoke test per tower that the effects reach the battle (the same "applied N of N" server log line the other effects use).

## Stage 2: twelve armor sets (built 2026-10-08)

Eight new sets (32 pieces) beside the four that existed: 12 sets, 48 pieces. Items are registered in Java (`ArmorSetItems.SETS`), bonuses are data (`armor_sets/*.json`), art is generated by
`tools/generate_armor_art.py` (placeholder, replaceable later without code; `tools/preview_armor_art.py` renders a contact sheet). Wearing rules, tooltips and mixing (2 and 4 piece tiers) are unchanged.

| Tower | Tier I, Utility | Tier II, Offense (existing) | Tier III, Defense |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neutral | Recruit's Gear: +6% EXP, +1 max health; 4: -3% damage taken, vendor -5% | Challenger's Regalia | Paragon's Aegis: +2 max health, +10% EXP; 4: lead +1 Defense, -8% damage taken, vendor -10% |
| Tideforge | Tidewalker's Garb: +1 breath, +8% catch rate; 4: rain 3 turns, +5% Raid Points | Tideforged Plate | Leviathan Mail: +2 max health, +10% catch rate; 4: rain 5 turns, -10% Water and Electric damage taken, +10% Raid Points |
| Rootvale | Sprout Garb: +8% EXP, +1 max health; 4: grassy terrain 3 turns, +5% Raid Points | Rootwoven Mail | Heartwood Bulwark: +3 max health, +10% EXP; 4: grassy terrain 5 turns, -10% Grass and Fairy damage taken, vendor -10% |
| Duskvale | Dusk Wanderer's Cloak: +1 luck, +5% shiny odds; 4: -5% Dark damage taken, +5% Raid Points | Duskbound Guard | Nightfall Carapace: +1 luck, +10% shiny odds; 4: lead +1 Speed, -10% Dark and Ghost damage taken, +10% Raid Points |

Item ids are `cobbletowers:<set>_<slot>` with set ids `recruit`, `paragon`, `tidewalker`, `leviathan`, `sprout`, `heartwood`, `duskwanderer`, `nightfall`. Every set carries all four bonus kinds, as the first four do.

**Drops (reward-table entries, no crafting):**
* Tier I pieces: `floor_cleared` weight 3 and `boss_defeated` weight 6 per piece, in the tower's own table.
* Tier II pieces: where they were (floor_cleared 2, boss_defeated 5); the season spotlight still doubles only these.
* Tier III pieces: the **F10 champion** bonus pool only, weight 12 per piece beside the guaranteed XL candy, God Pack Ticket and Raid Points (about one clear in two yields a piece with its three bonus rolls). F5 pays none.

**Tests:** `ArmorSetTest` (every set parses, follows the naming convention, covers all four kinds, has its icons, layers and lang keys, and drops where its tier says and nowhere else), `SeasonTrimAssetsTest` (all 48 pieces trimmable),
`armor_set_test.py` 18/18 and `armor_hooks_test.py` 14/14 on the rig (both now expect 12 loaded sets).

## Open

* The names, numbers and drop weights above are untuned guesses for the owner to adjust; they are all data.
* The art is generated placeholder; a real artist's textures replace the files in `textures/models/armor` and `textures/item` with the same names.
* A tier III set is stronger than tier II in numbers (decided 2026-10-08); read `masteryadmin counters` and the F10 drop rate before tuning.
